Transit apps keep getting heavier while the underlying question stays
small: when does the bus come? Bus.fyi answers it as a plain website —
the schedule for every bus stop and train station in the Chicago CTA
system, built from open GTFS transit data. No downloads, no cookies, no
tracking; it loads fast on slow connections and works in older browsers.

The site is live at [bus.fyi](https://bus.fyi). Source spans
[the original GTFS app](https://github.com/ericmikkelsen/bus.fyi) (2016)
and [the current rebuild](https://github.com/ericmikkelsen/bus-fyi-app).
